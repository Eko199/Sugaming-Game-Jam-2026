## 📖 About The Game

**Blind Brew** is a fast-paced alchemical experimentation game developed in 48 hours on-site at the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics (FMI), Sofia University, for **SUGAMING JAM 2026**—where it took home **1st Place**!

You are the resident potion maker for a band of dungeon warriors gearing up to slay a dragon. There is just one problem: **you’ve completely ruined your recipe almanac.** Impatient adventurers are lining up at your window, describing the potions they need in only a few cryptic words. With no manual to rely on, you must deduce what they want through trial, error, and pure alchemical intuition before time runs out.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay & Mechanics

### How to Play
1. **Gather & Toss:** Drag ingredients from your shelf and drop them into the bubbling cauldron.
2. **Control the Heat & Stir:** Dial in the exact temperature and stir the concoction the right way.
3. **Serve Under Pressure:** Bottle and hand over the potion before the customer’s patience runs dry!

### Key Features
* **📓 Learn by Brewing:** Every successful potion automatically documents itself in your almanac. Any recipe or effect you haven't discovered yet remains shrouded as `??`.
* **⚖️ Quality Matters:** Brewing isn't just about picking the right herbs—precision with your temperature dial and stirring technique determines potion quality and yields higher coin payouts.
* **💀 Wrong Recipes Bite Back:** Handing over a botched potion doesn't just lose a sale; it curses you with a random debuff! Watch out for dulled senses, garbled customer dialogue, or a completely shuffled ingredient shelf.
* **⏳ Race the Clock:** Each shift gives you **3 minutes** to earn as much gold as possible, with the pressure ramping up in the final 30 seconds.
* **🥄 Upgrade Your Gear:** Spend your hard-earned coins on better spoons to boost your payouts and brewing efficiency.
* **🏅 High Score Chasing:** Your best run is tracked and saved directly on the main menu.

## 📥 Download & Installation

### Play the Release Build
You don't need to build the project from source to play! Grab the standalone Windows executable directly from itch.io:
1. Go to the [**Blind Brew itch.io page**](https://mastergari.itch.io/blind-brew).
2. Download `blind_brew.exe` (~112 MB).
3. Run the executable and start brewing!

### Running from Source
```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone [https://github.com/Eko199/Sugaming-Game-Jam-2026.git](https://github.com/Eko199/Sugaming-Game-Jam-2026.git)

# Navigate into the project directory
cd Sugaming-Game-Jam-2026
```
*(Open the project folder in your game engine editor to run or build from source.)*
